# Yara / 雅乐 — Master Project Context

## Product

**Name:** Yara / 雅乐  
**Positioning:** AI Music Companion  
**Vision:** Music Lifestyle OS

**Slogan:**  
Music is what feelings sound like. AI helps you discover the music, the people, and yourself.

## What Yara helps users do

- Discover Music
- Understand Music
- Experience Music
- Collect Music
- Share Music
- Remember Music

## Product principle

**Music First. AI Everywhere, but Quiet.**

Yara should not feel like:

- Spotify + ChatGPT
- a generic chatbot with music prompts
- a music app with a separate AI tab bolted on

AI should be embedded into browsing, understanding, planning concerts, evaluating records, meeting people through music, and reviewing one's music life.

## Locked mobile navigation

- Home
- Explore
- Yara
- Community
- Me

## Locked PC semantics

- Home
- Explore Music
- Talk to Yara
- Community
- Me

## Core module mental model

- **Home** = the system proactively prepares today's music experience for me.
- **Explore** = I proactively browse the music world.
- **Yara** = I express a concrete intent and ask the AI companion to help.
- **Community** = I share, meet people, and participate in music-centered social experiences.
- **Me** = my long-term music identity, journey, and collection.

## Yara's irreplaceable core

The distinctive core is not “chat”. It is contextual AI Agent capability, planning and decision support, structured results, and long-term memory.

## Signature interaction

**Ask Yara about THIS**

Any music object can become AI context:
Track / Album / Artist / Playlist / Story / Concert / Festival / Venue / Vinyl / CD / Release / User / Music Moment / Journey.

## AI presence levels

1. Ambient
2. Assist
3. Conversation / Task

## Visual direction

Minimal / Editorial / Warm / Emotional / Intelligent / Music-first / Contemporary.
Desired feel: mature music product × editorial magazine × quiet AI intelligence.
Avoid robot icons, magic wands, AI orbs, blue-purple gradients, sci-fi HUDs, heavy glassmorphism, and “orange Spotify”.

## Brand color

Warm vivid orange around `#FF5A1F` as accent, not app background.

The project is currently at **Figma High-fi Design**.
